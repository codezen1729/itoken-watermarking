# Supplemental copyedit of the initially detectable pair

The primary cohort yielded one detectable marked original and its matched unmarked control. The first fidelity reader found a scope change in the marked rewrite, leaving no marked original eligible for the primary two-reader preservation analysis. We registered this supplemental procedure after seeing those facts and before looking at rewritten detector scores or making new texts. The primary rewrites and their outcomes stay in the original denominators.

Test every initially detectable marked original and its matched control. Give each source to a new blinded copyeditor. Ask for one light natural-language rewrite, with exact scope, conditions, actors and quantifiers and without requirements to keep named phrases. Use two fresh independent fidelity readers per text. Use fresh open extraction and the same frozen Qwen encoder, key bank, randomization statistic and 0.01 threshold. Report all attempts and all fidelity disagreements, even if there is still no eligible case. Compare the marked and control p-values. The result conditions on original detection, covers one deliberately selected problem, and tests lighter edits; it cannot establish accuracy across problems or rescue the primary result.
