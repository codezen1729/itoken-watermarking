Before drawing keys or scoring any main document, we added a second comparison of source and rewrite. A fresh reader receives the identical two texts and fidelity instruction, in their own context, and records the concrete meaning changes or the evidence of preservation. The original protocol's single-reader table stays available. For a stricter answer about meaning, we also report survival among initially detectable originals for which both readers independently judge all substantive reasoning preserved. A disagreement or uncertain judgment makes a pair ineligible for this secondary denominator. Both readers use the same model family. The source passages and judgments are retained for inspection.
