# Open reasoning-step recovery

Each of eight separately keyed public problems gets 24 free-written proposals from agents before key selection. Every proposal is a short argument with a reason, an action and an expected consequence. Generation may choose from this temporary sample. Recovery and detection receive no proposal bank, menu, reference step, expected step count or earlier version. A recovered step consists of three unrestricted text fields and a verbatim source citation. The schema is fixed and its vocabulary is open.

We draw each target key and 199 surrogate keys once. The original contains 12 of the 24 proposals in source order, joined into paragraphs of four. We use this direct composition to retain the original arguments. The control and marked selections use shared Gumbel variates. We rank each proposal by log base weight plus noise, adding four times a continuous keyed projection for the marked condition. Its base weight is Qwen's probability of the single token Yes, conditional on Yes or No, for a local inclusion question. This is a model-informed selection policy over generated samples, not a marginal probability over possible arguments. The author agents' native proposal probabilities are unavailable.

Before selection, a reader extracts free-form triples from each individual snippet. For detection, fresh readers independently extract as many or as few supported steps as they see in a whole document, with no IDs or count target. A public embedding takes the mean token state after the eighth transformer block of Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, uses a causal mask, L2-normalizes, subtracts the similarly normalized public problem vector, and L2-normalizes again. Zero contrasts contribute zero. The premise, action and consequence each receive their own SHAKE256-derived Rademacher direction under the key and problem. The step score is the mean of the three inner products. The document statistic averages scores over distinct supported triples. The same statistic is computed for 199 surrogate keys. A positive requires (1 + number of surrogate scores at least as high as the target score)/200 ≤ 0.01. No accepted steps yields abstention. The extractor works in prose and its steps need not agree word for word. This encoder and direction can respond to changes in words even when the reasoning stays the same.

Each original receives one free rewrite with no required vocabulary, labels or ordering. A separate reader compares source and rewrite without viewing recovered steps or scores. A pair counts as faithful only when every substantive relation, condition, actor, direction and scope survives, with no unsupported addition. Uncertain pairs are reported. A separate reviewer assesses each text and its extracted triples for coverage and support. These judgments are model-based. All eight marked and eight control pairs remain in primary rates. Report planned, original, rewritten, conditional survival and the subset of faithful, initially detected pairs. An empty survival denominator is undefined. The unit is a problem/key pair. No threshold, selector, encoder or sample size is adjusted after inspecting detection. This new intrinsic score differs from the earlier catalog detector, so differences between experiments cannot be attributed solely to removing the catalog.
