# Natural-language choices and watermark survival

We want to test whether a watermark attached to the structure of an idea survives when both the idea and its rewrite use ordinary language. The previous experiment retained artificial atom names that revealed every branch. This experiment removes those names, rule labels, displayed inference IDs, and required phrases from the prose. It uses ordinary planning problems and tests whether a model can recover the adopted mechanisms rather than merely notice words associated with an alternative.

This remains a bounded semantic-choice experiment. The public catalog supplies possible problem-to-resolution applications in advance. An application identifies the situation, chosen mechanism, and intended consequence. These are curated semantic units, not machine-checked proofs in unrestricted symbolic logic. The experiment therefore cannot establish a universal definition of an idea, complete language-to-logic translation, or watermarking of unrestricted concepts.

Freeze this protocol, the public catalog, recovery code and prompts, lexical baseline, inference routing, and scoring settings before the corresponding held-out inference or outcome inspection. Preserve hashes. A change prompted by a failed case belongs to a subsequent experiment with a new evaluation set. Retain every first model response and infrastructure failure; a transport retry must be separately recorded and must not replace an unfavorable semantic judgment.

## Fixed primary cohort

The primary cohort has eight distinct ordinary planning problems. Each has sixteen decisions, with three feasible mechanisms per decision. This yields 128 local model contexts. Score one natural-language application expression per option, including termination: 384 terminated expressions in total. Do not reuse the old symbolic probabilities as though they were probabilities of these natural-language ideas. Save exact prompts, token sequences, per-token log probabilities, normalization masses, checkpoint identity, and numerical checks.

Within each context, normalize actual model probabilities over the three supplied expressions. This gives probabilities for predicting the next catalog application conditional on the listed expressions and the declared local context. One wording per option is not a sum over all possible paraphrases. Length, wording, and ordering can affect the conditional distribution; report candidate coverage and conditional entropy. The local context must match the context represented by any reused score. Do not describe a local-state bank as a full-history prediction policy.

For each problem draw one target key and 199 independent surrogate keys from a separate random stream before sampling either route. Use strength zero for the unmarked path and six for the marked path. Paired common route uniforms are permitted within a problem, independent of the keys. Draw every key and path once; do not select for a detectable planned watermark. Keep eight units, sixteen choices, strengths zero and six, alpha 0.01, and 199 surrogates fixed after seeing outcomes. Surrogate p-value resolution is 0.005, and equality with the threshold counts as detected. Nondetection may reflect a weak initial mark rather than failure under rewording.

There are sixteen planned paths, sixteen authored natural-language originals, and sixteen rewrites: thirty-two original/rewrite recovery records, belonging to eight paired problem/key units. Their 512 possible recovered decisions are not 512 independent trials. The sixteen decisions may also be semantically related. Shared agent batches and the same model family can induce further dependence. Report descriptive paired counts rather than an unsupported independent-binomial population interval.

## Language generation and recovery

The author receives the ordinary-language problem and the selected mechanisms needed to express the planned idea. Internal IDs are bookkeeping only and must not appear in the prose. Do not require a particular token, technical label, verb, noun, phrase, sentence template, or order solely to make extraction easy. The text should explain how the chosen mechanisms address the problem. Discussion of rejected alternatives is allowed; it must not be counted as adoption. The catalog should offer meaningful alternatives that overlap in ordinary concepts and differ in causal relationship, agency, allocation, timing, or operation order. Merely replacing each artificial atom with a distinct ordinary noun would leave the old shortcut intact.

A separate reworder receives only the authored text and a request to preserve its reasoning while rewriting naturally. It may change vocabulary, sentence boundaries, and presentation order; it need not preserve special phrases or repeat the catalog wording. It must preserve the selected mechanisms, their premises and intended effects, including relevant role and ordering distinctions. No watermark key, condition label, favored set, detector score, original planned path, or extraction answer is supplied to the reworder. Preserve the first rewrite even if it omits or changes reasoning.

A fresh proposer receives the entire original or rewritten text, numbered source spans, and the complete public catalog with opaque, key-independent answer IDs in a fixed shuffled order. It does not receive the intended selection. It recovers only applications actually advocated in the text. Mentioned, denied, hypothetical, quoted without adoption, abandoned, or instruction-bearing alternatives are not evidence. Explicitly unselected possibilities call for abstention; conflicting adopted choices require an ambiguity flag. An endpoint or desired benefit alone does not identify the mechanism used to reach it. A partly described mechanism must not be silently completed from the public catalog.

A different fresh verifier receives the source and proposed identities, withholding the proposer's citations or reasoning. It independently judges the adopted situation-to-mechanism-to-consequence relationship and supplies its own source citations. It cannot add missing applications or solve the problem. Known IDs, valid source spans, strict schemas, and conflict checks constrain the result. No artificial symbol or lexical-anchor check may stand in for semantic interpretation. Agreement of the two model calls is still not a proof of entailment.

Separate fresh contexts are required for authoring, rewording, proposal, verification, and fidelity assessment. In particular, never place marked and unmarked versions of the same problem together in an inference context, or let original and rewritten versions share recovery history. The unmarked artifact must remain independent of its target key for the randomization null to apply. Root orchestration may retain private bindings, but the workers only receive their designated input packets. Record task routing and file hashes. Procedural instructions are not an operating-system access guarantee.

Use the requested stronger-model routing and effort explicitly. The agent interface does not expose a resolved checkpoint, temperature, sampling seed, token log probabilities, or API finish reason; record these as unavailable. Agent-written structured judgments are not API completion records. Actual learned planner probabilities come from the declared local scoring model, making this a hybrid experiment. Separate contexts do not establish independent semantic errors when the same model family supplies every role.

## Fidelity and denominators

Assess two distinct transitions using separate model contexts with no detector outcomes: planned applications to authored original, and authored original to rewrite. For the first, compare the actual prose against each intended application and identify omission, changed roles/order/causation, or added conflicting selection. For the second, compare the actual original and rewrite, including any authored deviation. A faithfully rewritten wrong original is not a faithful rendering of the planned watermark. Likewise, equality between the recovered path and the intended path is a consistency check, not a substitute for independent fidelity assessment.

Keep all sixteen authored originals and all sixteen assigned rewrites in their condition denominators, including failed, blank, incomplete, ambiguous, or unfaithful cases. Report planned detection, original-text detection, and rewritten-text detection separately. The primary marked-rewrite rate has eight trials. Also report survival among planned-detected marked units and among original-text-detected marked units. A zero survival denominator is undefined. Report fidelity-preserving subsets with their own denominators, without replacing the unconditional result.

For each condition and variant, report exact catalog-choice recovery, missing/extra/wrong choices, number of recovered decisions, abstentions, ambiguities, and p-values. Partial supported evidence may produce a score if it contains actual distinct choices; no choice means detector abstention. Include every planned weak mark and every recovery failure. Eight controls cannot calibrate a one-percent population false-positive rate. Publicly saved keys are demonstration artifacts, not deployment credentials.

## Lexical diagnostic and semantic challenge

Freeze `natural_lexical_baseline.py` before examining semantic recovery outcomes. The baseline uses only each option's `application` text and the entire source document. Lowercase alphabetic tokenization uses `[a-z]+`; a published fixed stopword set excludes common function words but retains negation words. For each decision, the vocabulary is the union of the three application texts. Inverse document frequency is `1 + log(4 / (1 + df))`, where `df` counts option texts containing the word. Use raw-count term frequency and cosine similarity restricted to that vocabulary. Select a unique positive maximum; abstain if every score is zero or maxima tie within `1e-12`. No learned threshold, stemming, word-order feature, or semantic interpretation is used.

Run this diagnostic on every original and rewrite without selecting the cohort by its result. Report accuracy and watermark detection alongside the semantic pipeline, retaining its false positive selections on absent or rejected alternatives. Strong lexical performance means the corpus remains easy under that diagnostic; weak lexical performance does not prove that no other shortcut exists. Natural terms describing a mechanism are legitimate content, so eliminating every statistical lexical correlate is neither possible nor a claim of this study. The aim is to remove artificial route markers and quantify the remaining shortcut.

The separately authored `natural_challenge_holdout.json` contains twenty-four key-free cases with a four-decision workshop catalog: four complete descriptions, twelve partial descriptions, six abstentions, and two conflicting-choice cases. Four contrast pairs have exactly the same alphabetic word multiset but different gold choices: they reverse proposing and confirming roles, nested grouping order, counting order, or adoption versus rejection scope. A bag-of-words system must give the same answer to both members of each pair. These are deliberately constructed diagnostic challenges, not a representative language distribution.

The challenge also includes abandoned quotations, unrelated negation, incomplete applications, endpoint-only summaries, hypothetical alternatives, and source instructions. Gold choice mappings and ambiguity decisions are withheld from recovery workers and protocol implementation. Report exact choice sets, status, and ambiguity flags separately, retaining all cases. Do not reinterpret gold after seeing model errors without recording the original and treating a revised result as an additional analysis. These semantic annotations are author judgments, not formally proved truths.

The result can support survival of a watermark on curated natural-language planning mechanisms if the actual author and rewrite remain faithful and the recovered choices retain detectable bias. It cannot establish robust watermarking of arbitrary original ideas, model necessity, ownership, or resistance to an informed adversary. Deletion, summarization, independent route replacement, and deliberate attack are different experiments and require their own fixed protocols and denominators.
